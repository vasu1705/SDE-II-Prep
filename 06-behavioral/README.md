This section holds **your** stories, so the story content below is intentionally blank: fill it with real events from your own work. Aim for 2–3 minutes spoken per story.

## STAR template
| Part | Say | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ituation | context in 1–2 sentences (team, system, scale, deadline) | include a number: traffic, users, revenue, deadline |
| **T**ask | what *you* were responsible for | "I", not "we" |
| **A**ction | 3–4 concrete steps you took and **why** | show judgement and trade-offs |
| **R**esult | measurable outcome + what you learned | latency down X %, incident avoided, time saved |

### 1. Trade-off under pressure — prompts to answer
- What was at stake and by when? What options did you have (cut scope, add capacity, degrade gracefully, feature flag)?
- What did you **deliberately not do**, and what was the risk you accepted? How did you mitigate it (monitoring, rollback plan, follow-up ticket)?
- Result and what you changed afterwards.

### 2. Project deep dive — be ready to defend
- Architecture diagram you can draw in 3 minutes; the 3 biggest decisions and alternatives you rejected.
- Hardest bug/challenge: how you diagnosed it, what you tried that failed, the fix, the prevention.
- Numbers: scale, latency, error rates, team size, your exact role.
- Pick topics from this repo you can discuss deeply, e.g. [async error handling & retries](../02-languages/java/03-concurrency/03-async-error-handling-http.md), [`@Transactional` pitfalls](../02-languages/java/06-spring-boot/02-proxies-transactions-async.md), [N+1 fixes](../02-languages/java/06-spring-boot/03-jpa-hibernate.md).

### 3. Conflict — prompts to answer
- What was the disagreement, and what was each side's actual interest?
- How did you listen, bring data, and reach a decision? What if it went against you?
- What did the relationship and the delivery look like afterwards?

### 4. Why this company — research checklist
- [ ] The product and who uses it  - [ ] Engineering blog/talks: stack and scale problems
- [ ] A specific technical challenge you want to work on  - [ ] How your experience maps to it
- [ ] 2–3 thoughtful questions to ask them

## Common follow-ups
"What would you do differently?" · "What was your biggest mistake?" · "How did you measure success?" · "Who disagreed and why?"
